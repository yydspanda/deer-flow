# Business Context In Network Evidence

Interpret business content as well as transport behavior. Look for application
names, service addresses and paths in payloads, then use relevant reviewed
enterprise knowledge and Memory to explain their purpose.

State whether this explains the detected behavior. Cite the current clue and the
knowledge used, or explain the specific mismatch. Trust reviewed business identity
without demanding duplicate confirmation. Applicable business knowledge may support
a benign verdict without a Memory directive; compare the current behavior and any
material counterevidence instead of treating missing directive authority as suspicion.

A payload mention is not a new network destination and must not change the observed
tuple. Do not infer blanket safety from a domain alone or ignore a material business
explanation merely because a detector used an attack label. Enterprise-specific
service identities and addresses belong in tenant knowledge, never in this method.
